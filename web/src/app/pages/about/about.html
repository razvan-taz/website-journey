About

Hey, I'm TAZ.

I'm an ex-tech support agent, current programming novice, and lifelong gamer who's most at home in strange worlds, chaotic fights, and the kind of late-night nonsense only gaming can produce. I stream because I genuinely love games, the atmosphere around them, and the fun of sharing that experience with other people.

You'll usually find me playing things like World of Warcraft, Warframe, Dune: Awakening, No Man's Sky, and Call of Duty: Warzone, but I'm always open to jumping into something new if it looks fun, weird, intense, or just plain worth the time.

This stream isn't about pretending I'm the best player in the universe. It's about good games, good laughs, learning as I go, and building a place where people can hang out without all the fake hype and nonsense. Expect a mix of wins, fails, questionable decisions, and real reactions.

So if you're into gaming, chilled chaos, and a community that doesn't take itself too seriously, you're in the right place.
